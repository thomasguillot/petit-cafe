Petit Café

A small menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake.

Download for macOS

Version 1.0.0. Apple silicon, macOS Tahoe 26 or later. Free and open source.

La Carte

Your Mac can sleep. Choose a café.

Every timed café ends on its own.

Le Comptoir

Petit Café lives in the menu bar. Click the cup, choose a café, and carry on. Or right-click it for À volonté.

La Maison

One menu.
There is nothing to set up. It launches at login unless you untick that in the menu.
It minds the clock.
A timed café ends when it should, so your Mac is never left awake by accident.
Like caffeinate.
What the Terminal command does, one click away in the menu bar.
It keeps to itself.
No analytics, no account. The only thing it fetches is a check for updates, which you can switch off.

Le Service

Petit Café runs on Macs with Apple silicon, on macOS Tahoe 26 or later. There is no Intel build. It is unsigned, so macOS asks before its first launch, once.

  1. Open the disk image and drag Petit Café to Applications.
  2. Double-click it. When macOS says it can’t verify the developer, click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway.

After that, look for the cup in your menu bar.
